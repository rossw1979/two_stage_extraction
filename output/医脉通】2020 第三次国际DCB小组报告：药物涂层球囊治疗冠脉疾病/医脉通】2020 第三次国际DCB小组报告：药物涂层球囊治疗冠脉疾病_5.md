**DCB DELIVERY.** First-generation DCBs were less deliverable than standard balloons with larger crossing profiles, whereas current-generation DCBs show improved deliverability. The passage of the most recently used pre-dilation balloon may be used as a guide, while the presence of very distal lesions, extreme tortuosity, or excessive calcification should all increase the awareness of the possibility of DCB delivery failure. The use of a guiding catheter with good support, a guide extension, a buddy wire, or, with particular care, deep guide engagement are all options to aid balloon delivery. Care should be taken when handling a DCB, as some brands may shed drug and carrier when touched or after contact with liquid.
