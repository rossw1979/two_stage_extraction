#### 图 8 Algorithm for antithrombotic therapy in non-ST-segment elevation acute coronary syndrome patients with atrial fibrillation undergoing percutaneous coronary intervention or medical management.

**自然语言概述**：  
该流程图针对接受经皮冠状动脉介入治疗（PCI）或药物管理的非ST段抬高型急性冠脉综合征（NSTE-ACS）合并房颤（AF）患者，根据出血风险与缺血风险分层，推荐不同抗栓策略随时间动态调整。纵轴为“自治疗启动起的时间”，分为：入院至1周内、1个月、3个月、6个月、12个月；横轴分为三类策略路径：默认策略（Default Strategy）、高出血风险（High Bleeding Risk）、高缺血风险（High Ischaemic Risk）。绿色代表Ⅰ类推荐，黄色代表Ⅱa类推荐。

- **默认策略**（绿色）：  
  - ≤1周（住院期间）：三联疗法（(N)OAC + DAPT，即NOAC + 阿司匹林 + P2Y₁₂受体抑制剂）  
  - 1–6个月：双联疗法（(N)OAC + SAPT，即NOAC + 单一抗血小板药）  
  - 6–12个月：双联疗法（(N)OAC + SAPT）  
  - ≥12个月：单用(NOAC)

- **高出血风险**（黄色）：  
  - ≤1周：双联疗法（(N)OAC + SAPT）  
  - 1–6个月：双联疗法（(N)OAC + SAPT）  
  - >6个月：单用(NOAC)

- **高缺血风险**（黄色）：  
  - ≤1周：三联疗法（(N)OAC + DAPT）  
  - 1–6个月：双联疗法（(N)OAC + SAPT）  
  - 6–12个月：双联疗法（(N)OAC + SAPT）  
  - ≥12个月：单用(NOAC)  

所有路径最终均过渡至单用NOAC维持治疗。

Rivaroxaban monotherapy was superior for the primary safety endpoint of major bleeding (HR 0.59, 95% CI 0.39–0.89).

In NSTE-ACS patients managed medically, available data support DAT over TAT, with a single antiplatelet agent (most commonly clopidogrel) for at least 6 months. In a registry, bleeding risk was increased on TAT compared to VKA plus a single antiplatelet agent at 90 days, but not at 1 year, without differences in ischaemic events. In addition, warfarin plus clopidogrel resulted in a non-significant reduction in major bleeds compared with TAT, with a non-significant reduction in MI or cardiovascular death. In the randomized Antithrombotic Therapy after Acute Coronary Syndrome or PCI in Atrial Fibrillation (AUGUSTUS) trial, approximately 23% of enrolled patients presented with medically managed ACS. In these patients, apixaban significantly reduced bleeding events vs. VKA (HR 0.44, 95% CI 0.28–0.68) and death or hospitalization (HR 0.71, 95% CI 0.54–0.92), while no significant differences were observed in death or ischaemic events (HR 0.71, 95% CI 0.46–1.09). Aspirin vs. placebo presented a strong trend towards higher bleeding events (HR 1.49, 95% CI 0.98–2.26), but no significant differences in death or hospitalization (HR 1.16, 95% CI 0.90–1.51) or ischaemic events (HR 1.01, 95% CI 0.66–1.55).

### 5.3.2 Patients requiring vitamin K antagonists or undergoing coronary artery bypass surgery

In patients where VKA is mandated (e.g. patients with mechanical prosthetic valves), DAT with VKA and SAPT (preferably clopidogrel) is indicated after a short in-hospital period of TAT (with aspirin and clopidogrel). Compared with TAT (consisting of VKA plus aspirin and clopidogrel), DAT (VKA plus clopidogrel) was associated with a trend towards a reduction in Thrombolysis In Myocardial Infarction (TIMI) major bleeding (OR 0.58, 95% CI 0.31–1.08) in a network meta-analysis, while no significant difference was observed in MACE (OR 0.96, 95% CI 0.60–1.46).
