Take home figure  

A large proportion of patients undergoing coronary angiography because of angina and evidence of myocardial ischaemia do not have obstructive coronary arteries but have demonstrable ischaemia. This entity is defined as INOCA.  

CMD alone or in combination with CAD, is a mechanism of myocardial ischaemia and symptoms in INOCA.  
Epicardial vasospasm, alone or in combination with CAD, is another mechanisms of myocardial ischaemia  

Non-invasive functional techniques are options to detect ischaemia in INOCA.  
ETT, TTDE, MCE, SPECT  
MRI, PET  

Invasive strategies using coronary angiography and interventional diagnostic procedure (IDP) should be implemented to differentiate between vasospastic angina, microvascular angina and non-cardiac pain.  
Diagnostic guidewire, pressure and flow measurements  
Pharmacological coronary reactivity testing  

A stratified approach to the management of INOCA to address the short and long-term well-being and prognosis is warranted.  
Tailored counselling on life-style factors, risk factor management as per CVD prevention guidelines  
Use of pharmacotherapy to alleviate ischaemia and symptoms  

Given the lack of in-depth knowledge, further research is urgently needed.  
To increase our mechanistic understanding  
To develop innovative therapies to better manage this serious condition
