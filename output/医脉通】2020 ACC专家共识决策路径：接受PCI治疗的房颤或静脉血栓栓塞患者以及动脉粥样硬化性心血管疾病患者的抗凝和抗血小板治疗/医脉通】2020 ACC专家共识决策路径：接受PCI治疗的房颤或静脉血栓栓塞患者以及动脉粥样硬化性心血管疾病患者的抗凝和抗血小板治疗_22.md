6. DISCUSSION  
The primary objective of this document is to provide a framework for decision-making among patients who require concomitant use of an AC and APT. This is a complex topic, and we have attempted to cite the literature to offer direct guidance when possible and to highlight areas in which clinical judgement is needed. We hope this document will aid in the management of this common yet challenging subset of patients.
